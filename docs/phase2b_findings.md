# Phase 2B findings

Phase 2B closes the known text-box coverage gap, freezes extractor schema 1.0,
adds a privacy-minimized manually reviewed development set, and implements
local context-qualified PERSON, COMPANY, and ADDRESS detection. It does not
pseudonymize the prospectus or create a redacted DOCX.

## Text boxes and shape metadata

The prospectus now extracts 4,772 unique logical containers: 1,006 body, 3,525
table-cell, 75 header, 74 footer, and 92 text-box paragraphs. All 92 text-box
paragraphs have equal DrawingML `mc:Choice` and VML `mc:Fallback`
representations and one logical container rewrites both branches atomically.
There are 91 non-empty text-box paragraphs. Mismatched synthetic pairs are
extracted visibly but fail closed on rewrite. The stable ID grammar and version
bump policy are recorded in `docs/extractor_schema.md`.

A privacy-safe package audit found 98 non-empty accessible descriptions and 98
Selection Pane names across 74 XML parts (86 unique values). The hybrid detector
found no candidate required PII in those attributes. These findings are not a
claim that arbitrary shape metadata is safely rewritable: descriptions/titles
can be exposed to assistive technology and names through Word's Selection Pane,
but `python-docx` does not model them as ordinary visible text. Phase 2B audits
and reports them without automatic mutation. Raster-image text remains outside
scope because OCR was explicitly excluded.

## Development ground truth

Before installing or tuning spaCy, 53 source containers were selected and
exhaustively reviewed: 17 address/contact, 13 contact-table, 10 management, 9
precision-negative, 2 capitalized-heading negative, and 2 text-box negative.
The 44 positive annotations comprise 14 PERSON, 13 COMPANY, 6 ADDRESS, 6 EMAIL,
and 5 PHONE spans. SSN, CREDIT_CARD, DOB, and IP_ADDRESS have zero real-document
positives in this development sample. Negative containers nevertheless test
ordinary dates, CIN/SEBI identifiers, financial values, role/headings, and
page references. Annotation files contain offsets but no raw entity values.

The manifest is tied to source SHA-256
`8b5c93f7642d659e64b51be9f6172c86c2825417f376ca1800ed331515e6f929`
and extractor schema 1.0. The loader refuses incomplete reviews, changed source
hashes, incompatible extractor versions, missing containers, duplicate spans,
and spans outside source text.

## Local semantic detection

spaCy 3.8.15 and `en_core_web_sm` 3.8.0 load successfully on CPython 3.13.6.
The model wheel is pinned by SHA-256
`1932429db727d4bff3deed6b34cfc05df17794f4a52eeb26cf8928f7c1a0fb85`;
the installed model package-tree audit hash, excluding generated bytecode, was
`790f7d5c235e1667bda3fce5cad75fbbb2958d5b147702024cd948d494602502`.
Inference is local.

- PERSON requires a plausible multi-token name proposed by PERSON NER, or an
  ORG mislabel backed by person-role/contact context. Slash-separated contact
  lists are split into independent spans. Generic role/headings and common
  corporate/legal words are excluded. Accepted confidence is at least 0.82.
- COMPANY combines high-confidence legal-suffix spans with ORG NER that must
  also have commercial or document-role evidence. Short/generic ORG spans,
  role words, person-role contexts, and unsupported all-caps headings are
  rejected. Legal suffix evidence is scored 0.97; accepted NER context is at
  least 0.84.
- ADDRESS requires a validated Indian postal code plus premise digits, at
  least two comma-separated components, and street/building/locality structure.
  Address labels, region components, and FAC/GPE/LOC NER raise confidence, but
  a location NER span alone never creates an ADDRESS. Accepted confidence is at
  least 0.88.

Structured validators still outrank semantic candidates in overlap resolution.
The semantic provider retains only a bounded eight-text LRU so the three
recognizers can share one parse without indefinitely caching document content.

## Development metrics and error analysis

After calibration, the 53-container development set has exact micro TP=44,
FP=0, FN=0; precision, recall, F1, and entity-set accuracy are each 1.000.
Exact per-type results are 14/0/0 PERSON, 13/0/0 COMPANY, 6/0/0 ADDRESS,
6/0/0 EMAIL, and 5/0/0 PHONE. The macro aggregate across those five supported
ground-truth types is also 1.000. One-to-one ADDRESS relaxed overlap at a 0.5
intersection-over-shorter-span threshold is TP=6, FP=0, FN=0 and F1=1.000.

These are deliberately labelled **development/calibration metrics**, not
held-out or final-document performance. The perfect result is unsurprising for
a small subset used to select rules and thresholds and must not be generalized.
Categories with no positives report undefined entity-set accuracy, not perfect
performance.

Before context calibration, raw spaCy exact matching recovered 7/14 labelled
PERSON spans and 7/13 COMPANY spans. The principal errors were slash-separated
people emitted as one ORG, Indian personal names emitted as ORG, tab-fragmented
company text missed by NER, `Formerly` included in company boundaries, partial
legal-suffix spans, and generic organizational/role phrases proposed as ORG.
Rules and regression tests address these observed patterns. No FP/FN remains in
the calibration set; broader manually labelled holdout evaluation is still
required. A privacy-safe full-document scan emits 243 PERSON, 492 COMPANY, and
25 ADDRESS accepted detections after cross-type conflict resolution. Those are
unreviewed candidates—not metrics—and the COMPANY volume is a material risk to
audit on the holdout set.

## Verification and boundaries

The Phase 2B evaluator implements exact per-category, micro, macro-over-labelled
types, entity-set accuracy, and one-to-one relaxed ADDRESS overlap. Tests cover
semantic context, false positives, slash contacts, suffix/boundary handling,
location-only negatives, precision identifiers, text-box pairing/rewrite,
metadata audit privacy, frozen source/IDs, and evaluation matching.

Remaining risks include small-model NER quality on Indian names, COMPANY
over-detection in legal prose, address formats without Indian postal structure,
adjacent-cell context not represented in the recognizer interface, unsupported
shape metadata rewrite, Word fields/embedded objects, raster image content, and
the lack of an independently held-out annotated evaluation sample. No supplied
DOCX was modified and no redacted output was created.
